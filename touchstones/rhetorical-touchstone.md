# Rhetorical Touchstone

*The quality standard for rhetorical/essay prose in this project.*

The canonical example is `Writing Projects/Zine/_rhetorical/02b-perception-form-trees/pine-bark-final2.md`.

---

## What makes it the touchstone

**Phenomenological observation flows into philosophical reflection without either collapsing into the other.** The piece stays in the body — pressing hand to bark, the fissures, ochre and rust-red — while simultaneously thinking at the level of form/matter, whole/parts, withdrawn vs. sensual objects. Neither register explains away the other.

**The concrete image is never abandoned.** The bark stays in the room throughout. Even the most abstract philosophical passages return to the physical encounter.

**The voice is consistent.** The register established at the opening (intimate, phenomenological, unhurried) holds through the philosophical elaboration.

**The structure is spatial, not argumentative.** The organizing logic is a single sitting with the tree. This means the philosophical claims emerge from the encounter rather than the encounter illustrating the claims — the distinction matters to the reading experience.

---

## What to check against it

When revising a rhetorical draft, ask:
- Is there a sensory anchor that stays present through the philosophical passages, the way the bark stays present in pine-bark-final2?
- Does the philosophical register explain what the image is already showing? If so, cut the explanation.
- Is the voice consistent from the first sentence to the last?
- Does the organizing logic hold, or does the piece drift from its mode (spatial to argumentative, temporal to associative) without earning the drift?
